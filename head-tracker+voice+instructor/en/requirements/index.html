Head Tracker+Voice+Instructor — requirements

Computer

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11.
  • Administrator rights are not needed to install.

iPhone

  • An iPhone with Face ID — iPhone X or newer.
  • iOS 16 or later.
  • The iPhone app (available on the App Store after release).

Connection

  • A cable between the iPhone and the PC, or
  • the iPhone and the PC on the same Wi-Fi network.

For voice commands

  • The iPhone’s built-in microphone is enough.
  • Language: English, French, Russian, German or Italian.

For the instructor’s voice

  • The instructor speaks with a Windows voice in your language. If it stays silent, add a voice in Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices.

Not needed

  • No TrackIR, no IR clip, no webcam.
  • No account is needed for head tracking, voice commands or the instructor.
